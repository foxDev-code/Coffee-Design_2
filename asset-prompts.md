# Photography provenance

All five assets were created with the built-in image-generation tool for this separate NOIR project. These are generated replacements, not frames taken from the original website. The selected PNGs were copied into this project's assets folder; the generated originals were retained. CSS crops and scales the images for the layouts.

## assets/beans.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: full bleed cinematic coffee website hero, landscape 1536x1024. Extreme close-up freshly roasted glossy brown coffee beans inside the clear glass hopper of a brushed steel coffee grinder. Circular clear glass curves frame the beans, metal grinder above, dark warm cafe backdrop, rich brown beans with realistic grooves, macro editorial food photography, warm gold highlights, shallow depth of field. NO text, logo, watermark, UI, borders or collage. Real photograph look.
